Simple Daily Steps

  • Use separate bins for wet and dry waste.
  • Avoid unnecessary plastic and carry reusable bags.
  • Compost suitable kitchen waste.
  • Never throw waste on roads, drains or open spaces.
  • Give recyclable materials to authorized recyclers.

Types of Waste

🟢 Wet Waste

Organic waste such as vegetable peels, fruit waste, leftover food and garden waste.

Examples:

Food scraps, tea leaves, leaves and flowers.

🔵 Dry Waste

Non-wet materials that can often be reused or recycled.

Examples:

Paper, cardboard, metal cans, glass and clean plastic.

🔴 Hazardous Waste

Waste that needs special handling because it can be harmful.

Examples:

Batteries, chemical containers, bulbs and certain electronic waste.

Remember: Do not mix hazardous waste with normal household waste. Follow local collection rules.

♻️ Recycling Tips

01
Separate Materials

Keep paper, plastic, glass and metal separate where possible.

02
Clean Recyclables

Empty and rinse containers before placing them for recycling.

03
Reuse First

Reuse bags, containers and other useful items before discarding them.

04

Choose reusable bottles, bags and food containers.

05
Compost

Compost suitable kitchen and garden waste instead of sending it to landfill.

06
E-Waste

Give old electronics and batteries to authorized collection points.
