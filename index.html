Types of Waste

🟢 Wet Waste

Organic waste that can decompose naturally.

Examples: fruit and vegetable peels, leftover food, tea leaves, flowers and garden waste.
What to do: Put it in the wet/organic waste bin. Suitable material can be composted and turned into manure.
Do not mix: plastic, glass, batteries or chemicals.

🔵 Dry Waste

Mostly non-wet materials that may be reused or recycled.

Examples: paper, cardboard, metal cans, glass and clean recyclable plastic.
What to do: Keep recyclable materials clean and dry and send them through appropriate recycling/collection channels.
Tip: Reuse items before throwing them away.

🔴 Hazardous & E-Waste

Waste needing special handling because it can be harmful.

Examples: batteries, bulbs, chemical containers, old chargers, phones and electronic equipment.
What to do: Keep these separate and use authorized collection or recycling facilities.
Safety: Do not burn, break or mix hazardous items with normal household waste.

♻️ Recycling & 3R Principle

1. Reduce

Buy only what you need and avoid unnecessary packaging.

Carry a reusable bag and bottle, avoid single-use items and choose products with less packaging.

2. Reuse

Use an item again instead of throwing it away.

Reuse jars as containers, donate usable clothes and repair items when practical.

3. Recycle

Send suitable materials for processing into new products.

Separate recyclable materials and keep them reasonably clean and dry. Follow your local recycling rules.

🗑️ Waste Collection Guide

Step 1: Separate wet, dry and special waste at home.
Step 2: Keep waste in suitable, covered containers or bags.
Step 3: Put normal household waste out according to your local collection schedule.
Step 4: Give e-waste and hazardous waste to authorized collection points.
Step 5: Never dump waste into drains, rivers, roads or open land.

Note: Collection days and bin rules differ by municipality. Check your local municipal authority for exact schedules.

🌍 Benefits of Proper Waste Management

Cleaner Streets

Proper disposal reduces litter and blocked drains.

Healthier Community

Better waste handling can reduce exposure to harmful waste and pests.

Resource Conservation

Recycling and reuse can reduce the need for new raw materials.
